FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Dipcos Energy do?

Dipcos Energy designs and supplies modular, scalable energy storage systems — LFP batteries, hybrid inverters and intelligent energy management — for homes, businesses, industry and critical infrastructure in Portugal and across Europe.

Is Dipcos a solar panel company?

No. Dipcos specialises in energy storage and intelligent management. Our systems integrate with solar panels, the grid and EV charging, but our focus is storing and controlling energy.

Does energy independence mean disconnecting from the grid?

Not necessarily. For most clients it means reducing dependency and gaining control: storing energy for later, using it when grid prices peak and protecting operations during outages.

What battery technology do you use?

Our modular solutions are based on LFP (lithium iron phosphate) battery technology, combined with hybrid inverters that manage solar, battery and grid in a single system.

Does Dipcos install the systems?

Dipcos does not perform installation directly unless agreed in writing. We provide technical guidance, verify compatibility, recommend qualified certified installers and coordinate the process.

How do I know which system size I need?

Book a free consultation. We analyse your consumption, operational requirements and goals, then size the system — from 0–50 kW residential setups to 500+ kW industrial installations.

Where does Dipcos operate?

Dipcos is based in Olhão, in the Algarve, Portugal, and serves clients in Portugal and across Europe.
