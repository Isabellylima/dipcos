About us

Energy designed for independence, efficiency and security.

Dipcos Energy is a technology-driven company with over 30 years of experience in energy efficiency and intelligent power solutions. We develop modular, scalable energy storage systems designed to adapt to real needs — from residential applications to complex industrial environments.

Our focus is simple: deliver reliable, efficient and future-ready energy solutions that provide autonomy, cost control and long-term value. No overengineering. No unnecessary complexity. Just energy that works — when you need it most.

Our story

Engineering solutions for a changing energy landscape.

As energy systems become more vulnerable and demand continues to grow, Dipcos exists to help homes and businesses take control of their energy. We combine technical expertise, intelligent design and advanced storage technology to create solutions that ensure continuity, resilience and sustainability.

From initial analysis to long-term monitoring, every Dipcos system is designed with one goal in mind: energy that adapts to you — not the other way around.

  1. Early 1990s

    Dipcos is created as a distributed power control system for boats and caravans, with multi-point control, real-time monitoring and fault detection over a low-cost optical fibre network.

  2. The years between

    Founder JP Ajami builds Global Fire Equipment, founded in Denmark and later established in Portugal — later acquired by a group under Halma PLC.

  3. Today

    Dipcos returns, focused on energy storage, control and system intelligence for homes, businesses and industry.
